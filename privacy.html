Privacy policy

Who uses it. Vault Reader is a personal tool used only by its owner, on mailboxes whose holders have explicitly agreed. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses. Read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete, label or change mail.

Where data goes. Mail is read on the owner's own computer and summarised into the owner's private notes. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access. Any account holder can revoke access at any time in Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
